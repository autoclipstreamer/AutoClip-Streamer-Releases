# AutoClip Streamer
**Local AI clip editor for streamers.** Turn Twitch, Kick and OBS streams into vertical 9:16 clips for TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels, all processed on your own PC.

1. Download and extract the ZIP (about 473 MB).
2. Run `AutoClip_Streamer` from the extracted folder.
3. Paste a Twitch/YouTube link or open a local recording, and export.
**Requirements:** Windows 10/11 64-bit, 4 GB RAM. An NVIDIA GPU is recommended.
## ✨ Features
- **100% local processing:** your videos never leave your computer.
- **No minute caps:** process 4-hour, 8-hour or unlimited streams.
- **Highlight detection** from audio peaks, vocal excitement and visual cues.
- **Animated karaoke subtitles** with word-by-word animation and emojis.
- **OBS multi-track audio** mixing (game + microphone).
- **Silence trimming** (jump cuts) with no subtitle desync.
- **QR mobile transfer** over local Wi-Fi (iPhone and Android).
- **Direct URL import** of Twitch clips, VODs and YouTube links.
- **16:9 supercut** compilations with automatic timestamps and chapters.
- **GPU encoding:** NVIDIA NVENC, AMD AMF, Intel QuickSync, with CPU fallback.
- **Export:** MP4 1080×1920 at 60 FPS.
